Ask a question
01What is Neuvex?

Neuvex is moldable software infrastructure. You expose your app's capabilities as permissioned, contract-bound primitives instead of a fixed UI. Your users' coding agents build custom interfaces and middleware against them inside a sandboxed runtime, while your data, auth and business logic stay under contract.

02What is a primitive?

A primitive is a permissioned, contract-bound unit of your app's capability. Its contract sets declarative allow and deny rules on data, actions and auth scopes, and Neuvex checks every call against it at runtime.

03How do I get started?

Annotate your existing API and handlers with Neuvex contracts. The neuvex init command scaffolds allow and deny rules on data, actions and scopes. To start building or book a demo, email operations@neuvex.cloud.

04Which coding agents work with Neuvex?

Any coding agent. Neuvex is agent-neutral: it is the neutral boundary between the agent and your app, so there is no lock-in to a single agent vendor.

05Can users change more than the interface?

Yes. Agents can attach governed middleware hooks, such as transforms, filters and routing, between primitives. Hooks are typed, sandboxed and reversible, and no unreviewed code ever runs against your core.

06What happens if a molded interface goes wrong?

Molded interfaces run isolated, so a bad mold breaks a view, never your data, and it can't reach your infrastructure or your data path. Every mold is versioned and diffable, and you can roll any interface back to a known-good state with one click.

07How does Neuvex protect sensitive data?

Field-level policies and PII redaction are enforced before any data reaches an agent or a rendered surface. Every primitive call is contract-checked and recorded in a full audit log, and you can revoke or re-scope any primitive at any time.

08Can we run Neuvex in our own infrastructure?

Yes. Run Neuvex in your own VPC or on ours. Your data path, your compliance boundary, your choice.
